Segmentation · M.Sc. thesis

Reading the coronary arteries

In X-ray coronary angiography, a narrowing (stenosis) has to be found, outlined, and placed in the right segment of the coronary tree. Together these feed the SYNTAX score, which helps cardiologists choose between a stent and bypass surgery.

My M.Sc. thesis works on this problem: stenosis detection in X-ray coronary angiograms, on the stenosis task of the ARCADE dataset, where this image comes from. In my PhD I want to take it further, towards models that stay reliable on small, rare narrowings and can tell when their own answer might be wrong.

Classification on imbalanced data

Finding the rare case

Medical data is rarely balanced. When one class is much rarer than the rest, a model can reach high accuracy by never predicting it at all, and miss exactly the patients who need it most.

I work on classifiers that stay reliable on rare classes and difficult cases: training that gives the minority class a real voice, and evaluation that counts how many rare cases were found, not only overall accuracy.

Explainability

Checking the reason, not just the answer

A confident answer can still be wrong. Here a classifier calls an early melanoma a harmless mole, and its explanation shows why: it looked at only one small, regular-looking part of the lesion.

I use explanations as a check, not a decoration. When an explanation does not cover the evidence, the prediction is flagged and corrected, so the model ends up right for the right reason.

About

Models that hold up on the hard cases.

I am an M.Sc. student in Artificial Intelligence and Soft Computing at the University of Tehran, working as a research assistant under the supervision of Dr. Hedieh Sajedi. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Tabriz.

I am drawn to medical imaging because it is a field where a model's mistakes have real consequences, and the hardest cases are often the ones that matter most. My research focuses on building deep learning models that stay reliable on these rare and difficult cases. Alongside research, I work as a Machine Learning Engineer, designing and deploying machine learning systems in industry.
